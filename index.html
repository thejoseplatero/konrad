03

What I would do

Lead teams through large digital projects. I scaled a group to 45+ people across four practices and built the manager layer under it. Getting a new team to its operating baseline used to take a quarter. It now takes days.

Run the strategy and design thinking workshops. I do this in front of paying audiences already, at BrainStation, at Queen's Smith School of Business, and at conferences in Toronto, San Jose, Miami, and Singapore.

Deliver through to implementation. I launched the Aeroplan member experience across web and app. Active membership doubled. Uber, Starbucks, and Marriott were added as earn and redeem partners. It won multiple Freddie Awards.

Bring AI into the delivery. I built the operating model where agents draft PRDs and user stories, prepare executive reporting, audit initiatives, and plan capacity. It is governed, repeatable, and used across my team. It replaced manual preparation instead of adding another tool.

Be a design and technology thought leader. I founded Compass, Air Canada's first global design system, one component set covering everything from customer apps to flight crew tools. It is now becoming a platform that AI tools can read and build with, so a prototype comes out on brand the first time.

Mentor the junior members of the team. I am an industry advisor and mentor in the Master of Digital Product Management program at Queen's Smith School of Business, and an instructor at BrainStation.
